Support

Email meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com. Include what you asked the assistant to do, roughly when, and what it said back. Please don't send meeting passcodes.

Hours and response time

Support is staffed 9am to 5pm Pacific time, Monday to Friday, except holidays. We reply to every message within 2 business days.

How to add, use and remove the assistant, with answers to common problems, is in Using it with Zoom.

Common answers

  • "Waiting for you to join the meeting": join the meeting yourself first. For a meeting on another Zoom account, the assistant can only follow someone in.
  • It's in the waiting room: the host needs to admit it.
  • Your connection lapsed: connect your Zoom account again; nothing else is needed.
  • Removing it: see Using it with Zoom.
