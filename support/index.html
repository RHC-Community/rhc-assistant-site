Support

Email meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com. Include what you asked the app to do, roughly when, and what happened. Please don't send meeting passcodes.

Hours and response time

Support is provided by community volunteers, 9am to 5pm Pacific time, Monday to Friday, except holidays. We aim to respond to every message within 2 business days.

For instructions on how to add, use and remove the assistant, and troubleshooting, see Using it with Zoom.

Meeting Assistant is run by RHC Community. Questions: meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com
