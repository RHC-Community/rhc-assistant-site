Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Meeting Assistant, run by RHC Community ("we"), keeps as little as it can: only what it needs to join a meeting on your behalf, and only for as long as it needs it. We don't store your name or your email address.

What we store

WhatWhy it's neededHow long
Your Zoom user IDTo know which connection is yours, and to remove it if you remove the app in ZoomUntil you disconnect, or 90 days after it was last used
Zoom access and refresh tokensTo ask Zoom for a one-meeting token when you bring the assistant into a meeting you're in
The meeting number you give it, and how the join wentTo join that meeting, and show you what happened30 days
The meeting's passcode, if you give oneTo get into the meetingOnly until that request ends

That's all. When you sign in, Zoom tells us your name and email; we don't store them. They're kept only in a cookie in your own browser, to show who's signed in and to check that your account may use the assistant. The cookie lasts up to 12 hours and is signed so it can't be altered.

We don't ask Zoom for access to your meetings list, recordings, contacts, chats or files.

In a meeting

Nothing from a meeting is ever stored. The assistant joins as a participant and doesn't record or transcribe audio, video or chat. It reads chat messages sent to it only to answer its commands.

How it's used and shared

  • Only to join the meetings you ask it to join, while you're in them.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared, except with the services that run the assistant (Google Cloud, Cloudflare and MongoDB Atlas, which hold or carry it on our behalf) or where the law requires.

Deleting it

Disconnect on zoom.rhccommunity.com, or remove Meeting Assistant in your Zoom account (see Using it with Zoom): your Zoom user ID and tokens are deleted at once. Meeting records expire after 30 days on their own; email us to have them deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or receive a copy. Email meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted, on access-controlled systems. Your tokens are used only by the assistant itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and significant changes are noted here.
