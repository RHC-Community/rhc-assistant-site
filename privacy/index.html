Privacy policy

Updated 13 October 2026

This policy covers the Zoom side of Meeting Assistant, run by RHC Community ("we"): what it gets from Zoom when you connect your Zoom account, what it does with it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect from Zoom

WhatWhyKept
Your Zoom user ID, account ID, name and emailTo know who connected, show you which account is connected, and match Zoom's notice if you remove the appUntil you disconnect
Zoom access and refresh tokensTo request a one-meeting token when you ask the assistant to join a meeting you're inUntil you disconnect; refreshed as Zoom requires
Meeting numbers and passcodes you give it, and how each join wentTo join the meeting you asked for, and tell you what happened30 days

We don't access your meetings list, recordings, contacts, chats or files, and we don't ask Zoom for permission to.

In a meeting

The assistant joins as a participant. It does not record or transcribe audio, video or the meeting chat. It reads chat messages sent to it, to answer its commands. [Confirm: nothing from the meeting chat is stored.]

How it's used and shared

  • Only to join meetings you ask it to join, while you're in them.
  • Not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone except as needed to run the service (our hosting provider, Google Cloud) or as the law requires.

Deleting it

Disconnect, or remove Meeting Assistant from your Zoom account (see Using it with Zoom). Your Zoom profile details and tokens are deleted at once. Join records expire after 30 days, or ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to receive a copy. Email [contact email] and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is kept on access-controlled servers and travels over encrypted connections. Tokens are only used by the service itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Significant changes will be noted here.
