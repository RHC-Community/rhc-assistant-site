Privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Meeting Assistant is a community service operated by RHC Community ("we"). Due to the anonymous nature of many of our community's meetings, we take privacy very seriously and follow Tradition Twelve.

Meeting Assistant stores only the bare minimum information needed to function in order to join a meeting on your behalf, and it only does so for as long as necessary. We never store your name or your email address, even though Zoom provides those when you log in.

What we store

What Why it's needed How long
Your Zoom user ID To know which connection is yours, and to remove it if you remove the app in Zoom Until you disconnect, or 30 days after it was last used
Zoom access and refresh tokens To ask Zoom for a one-meeting token when you add Meeting Assistant to a meeting Until you disconnect, or 30 days after it was last used
The meeting number, and whether the app joined the meeting successfully To join the meeting, tell you what happened, and allow for troubleshooting 7 days
The meeting's passcode, if you give one To get into the meeting Only until the assistant picks up the request, usually in a few seconds

That's all. When you sign in, Zoom tells us your name and email. We never store them at our end. They are kept in a your own browser on your computer, to show who's signed in and to check that your account may use the app.

We don't ask Zoom for access to your meetings list, recordings, contacts, chats or files.

In a meeting

Nothing from a meeting is ever stored. The assistant joins as a participant and doesn't record or transcribe audio, video or chat. It reads chat messages sent to it only to answer and process commands.

How your data is used and shared

  • The very limited data we collect is used strictly to join the meetings you ask it to join.
  • Your data is processed through the sevices used to host the app: Google Cloud, Cloudflare and MongoDB Atlas. All data is transferred and stored using industry standard encryption.
  • Your data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared in any way unless required by law.

Deleting Meeting Assistant

You can remove Meeting Assistant from your Zoom account at zoom.rhccommunity.com, or in Zoom Marketplace. Once you do, your Zoom user ID and tokens are deleted from our systems immediately. Meeting ID records are deleted automatically after 7 days, or you can email us to have them deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or receive a copy. Email meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over industry standard encrypted connections and is stored encrypted, on access-controlled systems. Your Zoom tokens and any meeting passcode are encrypted again by the app, with a separate key, before being stored in our database. When connecting the app, you sign in on Zoom's page, and we never see your Zoom password.

Meeting Assistant is run by RHC Community. Questions: meeting.assistant@rhccommunity.com
