Meeting Assistant

Meeting Assistant is a tool for use in peer support and community meetings. It joins a Zoom meeting to display the meeting's materials, keep time, and display pre-set announcements.

It is designed to be very simple to use, allowing volunteers doing service in a meeting to easily run the slides or timer, from any device, using only the in-meeting chat.

In meetings using the RHC Community Zoom account, it joins as host. It can also join a meeting hosted on another Zoom account, if a participant in the meeting adds the app to the meeting manually.
