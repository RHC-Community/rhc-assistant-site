Meeting Assistant

A meeting assistant for RHC Community's online meetings. It joins a Zoom meeting as a participant to keep time and show the meeting's materials, and answers a few chat commands.

It can also join a meeting hosted on another Zoom account, but only by following someone in: a person connects their own Zoom account, joins the meeting, and asks the assistant to follow. Zoom only allows that while they are in the meeting.
