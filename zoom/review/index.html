Test plan for Zoom Marketplace review

Meeting Assistant joins a Zoom meeting as a participant, through the Meeting SDK, to show slides, run a shared timer and post announcements, controlled from the meeting chat. In a meeting hosted on another Zoom account it can only join On Behalf Of a signed-in user who is in that meeting. This page walks through adding it, using it and removing it, and shows where each requested scope is used.

What you need

  • No test credentials. Use any Zoom account. Sign-in is with Zoom itself, and access is open to every Zoom user for the review.
  • Two Zoom accounts, ideally: User A connects the app and brings the assistant into a meeting; User B joins as an ordinary participant, to see what everyone else sees. One account is enough for everything except step 6.
  • The assistant's page: zoom.rhccommunity.com.

One meeting at a time. The assistant runs on a small server and can be in only one meeting at a time. If it's in use for a community meeting when you test, it can't join yours until that meeting ends. This is unlikely, but if it happens, please try again later in the day.

Scopes

  • user:read:user: read once at sign-in (step 1) to get the Zoom user ID. Only the ID is stored, to recognise the connection and act on deauthorization. Name and email are not stored.
  • user:read:token: used when User A asks the assistant to join (step 3), to request a one-meeting On Behalf Of token. The token is used at once and never stored.
  • user:read:zak and zoomapp:inmeeting: added automatically by the Meeting SDK feature and can't be removed. The app doesn't use them.

User A: add, use and remove the app

  1. Add (authorize). Open zoom.rhccommunity.com and press Sign in with Zoom. Zoom shows the permissions; press Allow. You're returned to the page, signed in, with a form to bring the assistant into a meeting.
  2. Start a meeting. With the same Zoom account, start a meeting (or join one hosted by someone else). Then, as host:
    • Let participants chat with everyone (in the chat panel, … → Participants can chat with → Everyone and anyone directly), which the announcements and the assistant's replies need.
    • Allow all participants to share their screen (Share Screen → Who can share? → All participants), which the slides need.
  3. Bring the assistant in. On the page, paste the meeting's invite link, or enter its meeting ID and passcode, and press Join. The page shows its progress. If you aren't in the meeting yet, it says it's waiting for you, for up to 10 minutes. If the meeting has a waiting room, admit Meeting Assistant.
  4. Announcement. Shortly after it joins, the assistant posts a welcome message in the meeting chat.
  5. Chat commands. Send these in the chat, to everyone or directly to Meeting Assistant. It answers by direct message.
    • help: lists the commands you can use.
    • timer: shows a countdown in the assistant's video. Its reply lists the controls: s starts or stops it, r resets it, a number such as 3 sets it to 3 minutes, and c closes it.
    • slides demo: the assistant shares a demonstration set of slides on screen. Its reply lists the controls: n for the next slide, b to go back, a slide number to jump to it, and s to stop. help lists the rest.
    • lock timer (host or co-host): only hosts and co-hosts can then work the timer. unlock timer opens it to everyone again.
  6. A second user. Have User B join the meeting and send help and timer: participants can work the timer unless it's locked. User B sending leave now is refused: from the meeting chat, only hosts and co-hosts can have the assistant leave. (User A, who brought it in, can always have it leave from the web page, as in step 7.)
  7. Have it leave. As User A (host), send leave; the assistant asks you to confirm, and leave now makes it leave. Or bring it in again (step 3) and press Ask it to leave on the page. It also leaves when the meeting ends, or once it's been alone for a while.
  8. Remove (deauthorize). Either:
    • On the page, press Remove Meeting Assistant's access to my Zoom. Its connection and tokens are deleted, and the tokens are revoked with Zoom. Or:
    • Sign in at marketplace.zoom.us, open Manage → Added Apps, and remove Meeting Assistant. Zoom's deauthorization notice deletes the connection and tokens.
    Afterwards it can no longer join meetings on your behalf, until you sign in with Zoom again.

Data

Stored: the Zoom user ID and the OAuth tokens, encrypted. A join request (meeting number and status) is kept for 7 days; its passcode is deleted as soon as the assistant picks the request up. Audio, video and chat are never recorded or stored. See the privacy policy.
