Using Meeting Assistant with Zoom

Updated 16 October 2026

Meeting Assistant operates primarily in meetings hosted on its own Zoom account. By request, we have expanded the app so that it can be added to meetings hosted on other accounts as well.

For that to work, someone who is already in the meeting has to add the app to the meeting each time. This page covers how that works, including connecting, using and disconnecting your account.

Note: Meeting Assistant currently runs on a small server and can only be in one meeting at a time.

Connecting your Zoom account

  1. Open zoom.rhccommunity.com and choose Sign in with Zoom.
  2. Zoom shows the requested permissions, click allow. (None of the optional permissions are needed, those are defaults added by Zoom.)
  3. You will then return to the Meeting Assistant page, signed in.

Meeting Assistant is a tool provided for use by RHC Community group volunteers. If the page says your Zoom account can't add it to meetings yet, email us or message on WhatsApp to ask for access. For any other issues, see Troubleshooting.

Using it

  1. First, join the Zoom meeting yourself. Meeting Assistant can only join meetings you are in.
  2. On zoom.rhccommunity.com, paste the meeting link (or the meeting ID and passcode) and press Join.
  3. Meeting Assistant then asks Zoom for a token to let it join the meeting on your behalf. It joins as a separate participant under its own name.
  4. Meeting Assistant can only join if you are already in the meeting. It will wait for you and keep trying for up to 10 minutes if you're not in the meeting yet.
  5. If the meeting has a waiting room, the host has to admit the app like any other participant.

In the meeting

After joining the meeting, if the main chat is open, Meeting Assistant will post an introduction saying what it is, how to use it, and to let participants know that it does not record any meeting content.

To use it, send commands in the meeting chat or via direct message to Meeting Assistant. The available commands will depend on the meeting settings, and whether or not you are a host or co-host.

  • help: lists the commands you can use.
  • slides: shares the meeting's slides on screen, such as readings or the meeting format.
    • Slides are only available if the host allows participants to share their screen.
    • Currently slides have to be pre-set for your meeting ID by the RHC Community team.
    • Once slides are displayed, the app lists the available controls, n for the next slide, b to go back, a number to jump to a specific slide, or s to stop.
  • slides demo: shows a demonstration set of slides
  • doc list: lists the library of community resources available to display in meetings.
  • timer: shows a countdown timer, for timing shares or speakers.
    • Like with slides, the reply then lists the controls: s starts or stops the timer, r resets it, a number sets the timer length in minutes, and c closes the timer.
  • lock timer or unlock timer (hosts and co-hosts): keeps the timer to hosts and co-hosts, or opens it to everyone.
  • leave (hosts and co-hosts): asks it to leave the meeting. Confirm with leave now.

Meeting Assistant will leave the meeting when you press Ask it to leave, on the web page or when a host messages it the leave now command.

Removing Meeting Assistant from your Zoom account

There are two ways to remove Meeting Assistant from your account. Both will immediately and permanently delete the connection:

  • On zoom.rhccommunity.com, press Remove Meeting Assistant's access to my Zoom.
  • In Zoom: sign in at marketplace.zoom.us, open Manage → Added Apps, and remove Meeting Assistant. Zoom tells the assistant, which deletes what it holds about you.

After that, it can no longer join meetings on your behalf and all records of your Zoom account ID are removed. See privacy for details of what is kept and for how long. You can connect again at any time.

Troubleshooting

  • "Waiting for you to join the meeting": join the meeting yourself first.
  • It's in the waiting room: the host needs to admit it.
  • It doesn't answer commands: check the meeting's chat lets participants message everyone or each other. Hosts can change this under Chat → … → Participants can chat with.
  • The slides don't appear: the host needs to allow all participants to share their screen (Share Screen → Who can share? → All participants), and nobody else can be sharing at the time.
  • Your connection lapsed: sign in with Zoom again.
  • Your account can't add the app to meetings: email or WhatsApp message us to ask for access.

For any other issues, see Support.
